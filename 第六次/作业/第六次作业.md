## $\color{purple}{\text{Part I. Problem}}$

$\text{Consider the Hamiltonian system :}$
$$
H(x,y)=-\frac12(x^2+y^2)-A_2x^3-A_2xy^2+(1-A_1)x^2y-\frac{3A_1+1}{3}y^3,
\qquad \dot{x}=H_y,\quad \dot{y}=-H_x.
$$
$\text{The bifurcation set is :}$
$$

$$
$$
\Sigma_2:\quad \{A_2=0,\ -3<A_1<1\}\ \cup\ \{3A_1^2-A_2^2=0,\ -\tfrac12<A_1<\tfrac32\}.

$\text{Goal : determine the phase portrait in each connected region of the parameter plane.}$

## $\color{purple}{\text{Part II. Equilibrium points}}$

$\text{The equilibrium equations are }H_x=H_y=0\text{ :}$
$$
\begin{cases}
H_x=-x-3A_2x^2-A_2y^2+2(1-A_1)xy=0,\\
H_y=-y-2A_2xy+(1-A_1)x^2-(3A_1+1)y^2=0.
\end{cases}
$$
$\text{The origin is always an equilibrium. For }x\neq0\text{, set }y=tx\text{. Eliminating }x\text{ gives :}$
$$
E(t)=A_2t^3-(A_1+3)t^2+A_2t+(1-A_1)=0,
\qquad
x=\frac{1}{-3A_2-A_2t^2+2(1-A_1)t},\quad y=tx.
$$

$\text{For each chosen parameter value, solve }E(t)=0\text{ and record the corresponding equilibria. Check separately for equilibria with }x=0\text{, especially on }A_2=0\text{.}$

### $\color{green}{\text{Type of an equilibrium}}$

$$
D^2H=\begin{pmatrix}H_{xx}&H_{xy}\\H_{xy}&H_{yy}\end{pmatrix}.
$$

- $\det D^2H<0$: saddle;
- $\det D^2H>0$: center (for a nondegenerate equilibrium of this Hamiltonian system);
- $\det D^2H=0$: degenerate equilibrium; analyze on $\Sigma_1$.

$\text{At the origin, }D^2H(0,0)=-I\text{, so it is a center.}$
