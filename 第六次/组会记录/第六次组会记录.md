### $\color{blue}{\text{cubic form , discriminent and multiple root}}$  
>$\text{Definition : }(cubic \; form)$  
>$\text{A real cubic form is like :}$  
>$$
>Q(x,y)=ax^3+bx^2y+cxy^2+dy^3
>$$  
>$\text{Where }a,b,c,d \in \mathbb{R}$  
>$\color{red}{\text{Property of cubic form : }}$  
>$\color{red}{\text{The following is equivalent when }y \neq 0 : }$  
>$$
>\color{red}{Q(x,y)=0 \iff at^3+bt^2+ct+d=0 }
>$$  
>$\color{red}{\text{ where }t\coloneqq \frac{x}{y}}$  

>$\text{Definition : }(\Sigma_3)$  
>$\text{Given a cubic form with parmeter }\lambda \coloneqq (\alpha_1 ,\alpha_2 ,...):$  
>$$
>Q(x,y)=a(\lambda)x^3+b(\lambda)x^2y+c(\lambda)xy^2+d(\lambda)y^3
>$$  
>$\Sigma_3 \coloneqq \{\lambda : \text{two lines in }Q(x,y)=0\text{ coincide}\}$  
>$\color{green}{\text{Remark : }}$  
>$\color{green}{\text{you can look it as : } at^3+bt^2+ct+d \text{ has multiple root with }t = \frac{x}{y}}$  

>$\text{Discriminent in cubic : }$  
>$\text{In quatratic case we know that the discriminent is }\Delta = b^2 - 4ac .$  
>$\text{Actually in cubic case , we also has discriminent as following :}$  
>$\text{Given }q(t)=at^3+bt^2+ct+d=0$  
>$\text{The discriminent of }q\text{ is :}$  
>$$
>\Delta = 18abcd-4b^3d+b^2c^2-4ac^3-27a^2d^2
>$$  
>$\color{green}{\text{We have the following table about roots :}}$  
>|Discriminent|How many roots in $q(t)$|How many real lines in $Q(x,y)$|
>|:--|:--|:--|
>|$\Delta >0$|3 different real roots |3 different real lines|
>|$\Delta =0$|has multiple roots|2 or 1 different real lines|
>|$\Delta <0$|1 real root , a pair of conjugate complex roots|1 real lines|
